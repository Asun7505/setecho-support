Frequently asked questions

Where are my workouts saved?

SetEcho saves your records on the device you use. The web preview saves them in that browser. To move your records, export a JSON backup on the original device and import it on the other device.

How do I back up or import my workouts?

Open Settings → Backup to export a JSON file. On another device or after reinstalling, open the same menu and choose the backup file to merge it into your personal log. SetEcho can also import compatible GymFixLab backups.

How do I remove my training data?

Open a workout or saved draft, expand the workout details, and choose the delete option. Delete the app or clear SetEcho’s browser site data to remove all local records. Delete any exported backups you want removed separately.

What should I do before reinstalling?

Export a backup first and make sure you can open the file from a location outside SetEcho. After reinstalling, use Settings → Backup to import it.

How do I find my version and build number?

If you installed the beta, open SetEcho’s page in TestFlight to find the app version and build number. On your iPhone, Settings → General → About shows your device model and iOS version.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open SetEcho Pro in the iOS app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you used to make the purchase. To recover your workout history, import your training backup separately.

Will testing SetEcho Pro charge me?

If your TestFlight build offers an Apple purchase, it runs in Apple’s sandbox and you won’t be charged. Sandbox purchases apply only to testing.
