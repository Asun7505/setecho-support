Privacy Policy

How SetEcho handles training records, backups, purchases, and support requests.

On this page

Data stored on your device

SetEcho works without an account. The app stores your workouts, drafts, routines, custom exercises, training goals, and preferences on your device. The web preview stores these records in your browser.

Workout entries can include dates, weights, repetitions, reps in reserve, and notes. If you use the strength assessment, you can enter your body weight, age, comparison group, training experience, and lifts. SetEcho uses this information to show your workout history, progress, and comparisons.

SetEcho keeps training records and strength calculations on your device. SetEcho has no built-in cloud sync for training records.

Backups and removal

In Settings → Backup, you can export a JSON file and choose where to save or share it. The file may include your training records, strength assessment data, and preferences. When you import a file, the app reads the selected backup and merges the data on your device.

SetEcho keeps your local training data between sessions. You can delete an individual workout or draft in SetEcho. To remove all local app data, delete the app or clear SetEcho’s site data in your browser. Exported files, shared copies, and any device backups are managed separately.

In-app purchases

Apple processes iOS in-app payments through the App Store. SetEcho does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password.

SetEcho uses RevenueCat to verify purchases, manage Pro access, and restore eligible purchases. RevenueCat generates a random, anonymous App User ID for this purpose. It may process this identifier, App Store product identifiers, receipts or transaction details, and technical information such as device type, operating system, and app version.

SetEcho uses this purchase information to provide Pro access and review purchase activity. Your training records and the contents of your backups are separate and aren’t sent to RevenueCat.

This site and external links

Visiting this support site does not upload your training records. The hosting provider may process connection information, such as an IP address, to serve and protect the site. If you follow a link to Apple or a research source, that site handles your visit under its own policies.

Support and privacy requests

If you email support, we receive your email address and any information you choose to send. We use it to respond and follow up on your request. You can email us with privacy questions or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we process about you.
