Pro purchases and refunds

The App Store shows the price and purchase terms before you confirm. Apple processes your payment and handles refund requests.

To restore eligible Pro access, use Restore Purchases in SetEcho while signed in to the Apple Account you used to buy it. To recover your training records, import your backup separately.

  • Request a refund from Apple

Training results and estimates

SetEcho helps you log strength training, compare workouts, and set training goals. Strength estimates and suggestions depend on the records you enter and the comparison group you select. Use them as fitness information when choosing loads and exercises suited to your experience.

For questions about pain, injury, or a medical condition, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Data sources

General gym comparisons use published data from GymStreak users. Competition comparisons use OpenIPF meet results published by OpenPowerlifting. In SetEcho, the strength assessment explains the scoring method, comparison groups, and limitations. Open Settings → About for source and license links.

  • GymStreak strength data
  • OpenPowerlifting data
